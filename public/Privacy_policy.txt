# Privacy Policy

At **ExoQuest**, accessible from our website and platform, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors and users.

This Privacy Policy document explains what information may be collected by ExoQuest, how we use that information, and how we protect it.

## 1. Information We Collect

When you create an ExoQuest account or use our platform, we may collect information such as:

* Username.
* Name, if provided.
* Email address.
* Password or authentication information.
* Team information.
* Competition participation information.
* Challenge submissions.
* Scores and leaderboard information.
* Profile information.
* Messages or feedback sent to us.
* Technical information about your device and browser.
* IP address.
* Log information.
* Information relating to platform activity.

The information you provide and the reason it is collected will generally be made clear at the point where we request it.

## 2. Information You Provide

If you contact us directly, we may receive additional information such as:

* Your name.
* Email address.
* Phone number, if provided.
* Contents of your message.
* Screenshots or other information you provide while reporting an issue.
* Any other information you voluntarily provide.

We recommend that you do not include unnecessary sensitive personal information in public profiles, challenge submissions, comments, or support requests.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information we collect to:

* Create and manage user accounts.
* Provide and operate the ExoQuest platform.
* Allow users to participate in CTF competitions.
* Process challenge submissions.
* Maintain leaderboards and rankings.
* Track achievements and competition progress.
* Improve, personalize, and expand the platform.
* Understand and analyze how users interact with ExoQuest.
* Detect cheating, fraud, abuse, and unauthorized activity.
* Protect the security of ExoQuest and its users.
* Troubleshoot technical problems.
* Respond to support requests.
* Communicate with users about the platform.
* Send important service-related notifications.
* Develop new features and functionality.
* Comply with applicable legal obligations.

## 4. Log Files

ExoQuest may follow a standard procedure of using log files.

Information collected through log files may include:

* IP addresses.
* Browser type.
* Internet Service Provider.
* Date and time of access.
* Referring and exit pages.
* Pages or features accessed.
* Challenge-related activity.
* Other technical information.

This information may be used for administration, security, troubleshooting, analytics, and detecting abuse.

## 5. Cookies

ExoQuest may use cookies and similar technologies to improve the user experience.

Cookies may be used for:

* Authentication.
* Session management.
* User preferences.
* Security.
* Analytics.
* Platform functionality.

Users may be able to control cookies through their browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some functionality of the platform.

## 6. Public Information

Certain information may be publicly visible when you use ExoQuest.

Depending on the features you use, this may include:

* Username.
* Team name.
* Score.
* Rank.
* Competition achievements.
* Badges or awards.
* Public profile information.

Please avoid using personally identifying information as your public username if you do not want it displayed publicly.

## 7. Challenge and Competition Data

When participating in ExoQuest challenges or competitions, we may collect information related to your activity, including:

* Challenge attempts.
* Flag submissions.
* Submission timestamps.
* Scores.
* Challenge completion status.
* Competition rankings.
* Team participation.
* Competition-related events and logs.

This information may be used to operate competitions, calculate scores, detect cheating, investigate disputes, and maintain platform security.

## 8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information stored by ExoQuest from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure.

We cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or stored by the platform.

## 9. Third-Party Services

ExoQuest may use third-party services for purposes such as:

* Authentication.
* Hosting.
* Analytics.
* Email delivery.
* Payment processing.
* Security monitoring.
* Infrastructure management.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party services you use through ExoQuest.

## 10. Data Retention

We may retain account, competition, security, and technical information for as long as reasonably necessary to:

* Provide the platform.
* Maintain competition records.
* Maintain security logs.
* Prevent abuse and fraud.
* Resolve disputes.
* Meet legal or regulatory obligations.
* Maintain legitimate business records.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

## 11. Children's Privacy

ExoQuest does not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to ExoQuest without appropriate authorization, please contact us so that we can review and take appropriate action.

## 12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including rights to:

* Request access to personal information we hold about you.
* Request correction of inaccurate information.
* Request deletion of certain information.
* Request restriction of certain processing.
* Object to certain processing.
* Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

Requests may be subject to legal, security, competition, or operational requirements.

To exercise applicable rights, contact us using the contact information provided below.

## 13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

ExoQuest may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

Any changes will be published on this page along with an updated "Last Updated" date.

We encourage users to periodically review this Privacy Policy to remain informed about how information is handled.
